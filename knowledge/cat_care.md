# Cat Daily Care Guide

## Feeding
Adult cats do well on two or more small measured meals a day. Kittens need three to four meals. Cats are prone to weight gain indoors, so measure portions.

## Water
Many cats drink more from wide bowls or fountains. Refresh water daily.
TASK: Refresh water bowl | feeding | 5 | high | daily | min_age=0

## Litter box
Scoop the litter box at least once a day and fully change the litter weekly. A good rule is one box per cat plus one. Straining in the box or going outside it can signal a urinary problem; a male cat straining without producing urine is an emergency.
TASK: Scoop litter box | hygiene | 5 | high | daily | min_age=0
TASK: Full litter change | hygiene | 15 | medium | weekly | min_age=0

## Play and enrichment
Indoor cats need two or more short play sessions a day, around 10 to 15 minutes each, using wand toys or chase games that mimic hunting. Play before meals works well because it follows the natural hunt, eat, groom, sleep cycle.
TASK: Wand toy play | enrichment | 15 | medium | daily | min_age=0

## Senior cats
Cats over about 11 years benefit from vet checkups twice a year, softer food if teeth are sore, and low-sided litter boxes.
