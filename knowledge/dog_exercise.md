# Dog Exercise Guide

## How much exercise adult dogs need
Most healthy adult dogs need 30 to 120 minutes of activity every day. High-energy working and sporting breeds such as Labradors, Border Collies, Huskies and Retrievers sit at the top of that range. Splitting activity into two walks, one in the morning and one in the evening, keeps energy and bathroom breaks regular.

## Puppies
Puppies tire quickly and their joints are still growing. A common rule of thumb is about five minutes of structured walking per month of age, up to twice a day, plus free play. Avoid long runs and repeated jumping until growth plates close.

## Senior dogs
Dogs over about 8 years usually do better with shorter, gentler walks spread across the day rather than one long outing. Watch for stiffness, limping, or reluctance, and slow down on hot days.

## Hot weather
On hot days walk early in the morning or late in the evening. If pavement is too hot to hold the back of your hand on for seven seconds it is too hot for paws. Bring water and shorten the walk.

## Mental exercise
Training games, sniff walks and puzzle feeders tire dogs out as much as running does. Ten minutes of training practice a day improves recall and reduces boredom behaviors like chewing.
