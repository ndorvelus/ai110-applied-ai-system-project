# Dog Feeding Guide

## Meal frequency
Adult dogs usually eat two measured meals a day, roughly 8 to 12 hours apart. Puppies under six months need three to four smaller meals. Measuring food with a cup or scale, instead of free feeding, is the easiest way to prevent weight gain.

## Water
Fresh water should always be available. Refresh and wash the bowl daily. A sudden increase in thirst can be a sign of illness and is worth mentioning to a veterinarian.

## Treats
Treats should be no more than about 10 percent of daily calories. Use small pieces during training.

## Exercise and meals
Wait about an hour after a big meal before vigorous exercise, especially for large deep-chested breeds, to lower the risk of bloat.
