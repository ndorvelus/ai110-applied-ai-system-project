# Grooming Guide

## Brushing
Long-haired dogs and cats benefit from brushing several times a week to prevent mats. Short-coated pets can be brushed weekly. Brushing is also a good time to check for lumps, ticks, and skin problems.
TASK: Brush coat | grooming | 15 | low | weekly | min_age=0

## Nails
Most dogs need nail trims every three to four weeks; if you hear nails clicking on the floor they are too long. Cats benefit from scratching posts and trims every few weeks.

## Teeth
Daily tooth brushing with pet-safe toothpaste is the gold standard for dental health in both dogs and cats. Never use human toothpaste, which can contain xylitol.
TASK: Brush teeth | grooming | 5 | medium | daily | min_age=1

## Bathing
Dogs generally need a bath only every one to three months unless they get dirty. Cats rarely need baths.
