# Medication and Preventive Care

## Giving medication on schedule
Give prescribed medication at the same time each day and log each dose so doses are not doubled or missed. Pairing a dose with a meal makes it easier to remember unless the label says to give it on an empty stomach. Dosing decisions belong to your veterinarian; do not change a dose without asking them.

## Parasite prevention
Monthly heartworm, flea and tick prevention is recommended for most dogs, and flea prevention for most cats, including indoor cats. Mark the date on a calendar.

## Checkups
Healthy adult pets should see a veterinarian once a year; seniors twice a year. Keep vaccination records together.
