# Rabbit Care Guide

## Diet
About 80 percent of a rabbit's diet should be unlimited fresh grass hay such as timothy hay, plus a handful of leafy greens and a small amount of pellets. Hay keeps teeth worn down and the gut moving.
TASK: Refill hay and greens | feeding | 10 | high | daily | min_age=0

## Exercise
Rabbits need at least three to four hours a day outside the cage in a safe, rabbit-proofed area.
TASK: Supervised free roam | exercise | 30 | high | daily | min_age=0

## Warning signs
A rabbit that stops eating or stops producing droppings for more than 12 hours may have GI stasis, which is an emergency.
