# Toxic Foods and Household Dangers

## Foods toxic to dogs
Chocolate, grapes and raisins, xylitol (a sweetener in sugar-free gum and some peanut butters), onions, garlic, macadamia nuts, alcohol, and caffeine are toxic to dogs. Dark chocolate and baking chocolate are the most dangerous. If a dog eats any of these, contact a veterinarian or the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center (888-426-4435) right away rather than waiting for symptoms.

## Foods and plants toxic to cats
Lilies are extremely toxic to cats: even pollen or water from the vase can cause kidney failure. Onions, garlic, chocolate, alcohol, and grapes are also dangerous. Many essential oils are toxic to cats.

## Medications
Never give human pain medications such as ibuprofen, naproxen, or acetaminophen to pets. Acetaminophen is especially deadly for cats. Only give medication a veterinarian has prescribed, at the dose they prescribed.

## Emergency signs
Seek emergency veterinary care for difficulty breathing, seizures, collapse, a swollen hard belly with unproductive retching, suspected poisoning, uncontrolled bleeding, or a male cat straining to urinate.
